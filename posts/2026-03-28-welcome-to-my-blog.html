Welcome To My Blog

2026-03-28 · 1 min read ·
welcome

This is a blog. I know, big shocker. So revolutionary. *insert sarcasm tag here*.

I am Gwynne, The Curious Hermit. This is not my first blog, but it is my first blog in several years. And it's nothing fancy. It's some html files, a stylesheet, and some JavaScript to handle the dynamic parts - like updating the blog index every time I add a new post.

I'm not sure yet how often I'll post here, but I do intend to post here more regularly than I have in the past. I have a lot of thoughts and ideas that I want to share, and this seems like a good place to do it.

I'd actually been building the structure of this site before I decided to use GitHub Pages as my hosting solution. That was cemented by getting admitted to the GitHub Education program, which came with some perks like a free domain and free SSL that made it a no brainer for my hosting. I got that becuase I'm a student learning to code.

I am not a traditional student.

I'm 47 years old and finishing a philosophy degree I started two decades ago. Other than a single intro to programming class I'm taking as a gen ed credit this semester, all my coding learning is a sidequest. The philosophy training turns out to be a big boon for learning to code. As does having built html/css pages way back in the Geocities days.

In addition to the blog posts here on the blog, this site will also serve as a portfolio for my various projects and experiments. I create apps that I want to use and games that I want to play, but I'm a newbie coder, so it's things like terminal habit trackers and text-based adventure games, gradually expanding to learning Godot for basic games to share with others.

More to come, so stay tuned!
